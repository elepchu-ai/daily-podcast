## [vi] Tin tức trong nước

Xin chào Elep, chào mừng bạn đến với bản tin buổi sáng thứ Hai, ngày 12 tháng 10 năm 2026. Chúc bạn một tuần mới thuận lợi, và mình bắt đầu từ những tin trong nước.

Trước hết là đối ngoại. Chủ tịch Quốc hội Trần Thanh Mẫn đã bắt đầu chuyến thăm chính thức Trung Quốc từ ngày 11 đến 15 tháng 10. Đoàn đến Bắc Kinh vào trưa Chủ nhật theo giờ địa phương, và được Phó Ủy viên trưởng Ủy ban Thường vụ Nhân đại toàn quốc Vương Đông Minh đón tại sân bay. Đây là chuyến thăm Trung Quốc đầu tiên của ông trên cương vị Chủ tịch Quốc hội. Trọng tâm là việc đồng chủ trì phiên họp lần thứ hai của Ủy ban hợp tác giữa Quốc hội Việt Nam và Nhân đại Trung Quốc, nhằm củng cố tin cậy chính trị và thúc đẩy hợp tác nghị viện.

Sang kinh tế. Theo số liệu sơ bộ của Cục Thống kê, tăng trưởng tổng sản phẩm trong nước quý ba đạt 9,95 phần trăm so với cùng kỳ, cao hơn mức 8,15 phần trăm của quý một và 8,81 phần trăm của quý hai. Tính chung chín tháng, kinh tế tăng 9,01 phần trăm. Khu vực công nghiệp và xây dựng tăng 12,5 phần trăm trong quý ba, dịch vụ tăng 9,45 phần trăm. Một số chuyên gia nhận định, để đạt mục tiêu tăng trưởng 10 phần trăm cả năm, quý bốn cần tăng khoảng 12,5 phần trăm, một áp lực không nhỏ.

Về thương mại, xuất khẩu chín tháng đạt 434,3 tỷ đô la Mỹ, tăng 24,5 phần trăm, trong khi nhập khẩu đạt 453,7 tỷ đô la, tăng 36,7 phần trăm. Riêng tháng chín, Việt Nam xuất siêu 1,27 tỷ đô la, nhưng tính cả chín tháng vẫn nhập siêu gần 19,4 tỷ đô la. Vốn đầu tư nước ngoài đăng ký đạt 50,36 tỷ đô la, tăng 76,4 phần trăm. Sản xuất công nghiệp tăng 12,3 phần trăm, còn chỉ số giá tiêu dùng bình quân tăng 4,52 phần trăm.

Chuyển sang thị trường. Phiên cuối tuần trước, ngày 9 tháng 10, VN-Index đóng cửa ở 1.735,09 điểm, giảm 3,88 điểm, tương đương 0,22 phần trăm. Về vàng, ngày 10 tháng 10, vàng miếng SJC đi ngang ở mức 141,8 triệu đồng một lượng mua vào và 144,8 triệu đồng bán ra. Tỷ giá trung tâm sáng nay là 25.626 đồng đổi một đô la.

## [en] World news

Now to the world news. Starting with technology.

OpenAI has rolled its GPT-6 models into the main ChatGPT experience. Starting October 7, paid users got GPT-6 Sol, and free and Go users received the lighter GPT-6 Luna a day later. Both replace the GPT-5.6 versions in ChatGPT. The update also adds what OpenAI calls an intelligent interface, where answers can include interactive charts, forms and buttons, and partial answers appear earlier. In the developer API, Sol costs two dollars per million input tokens and ten dollars per million output tokens, while Luna costs just ten cents and fifty cents. Why it matters: a cheap, capable model for free users, and aggressive pricing for developers, raises pressure on every rival.

Google answered at its Gemini at Work event on October 8, introducing the Gemini agent. It is a coworker-style assistant that gets its own Workspace account. Colleagues can mention it in Chat or tag it in a document comment, and it can plan work that runs for hours or even days in the cloud, routing tasks across Gemini and Anthropic's Claude models. It is only in private preview for selected customers, and Google has given no price or general release date. The significance is the shift from chatbots you talk to, toward software teammates that carry tasks through on their own.

Turning to geopolitics. Saudi Arabia says a Houthi missile strike on King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh on Saturday killed at least 12 people and wounded more than 300. India's embassy says one of its citizens later died of injuries, which would bring the toll to 13. It is described as the deadliest attack in the kingdom in more than a decade. The airport was struck again on Sunday, causing a fire in Terminal 4, and more than 600 flights were cancelled. The Saudi-led coalition called the attack a war crime, and President Donald Trump said he was considering joining Saudi strikes on the Houthis in Yemen. The strikes come as a United Nations brokered truce in Yemen, largely in place since 2022, has collapsed. The Houthis have captured stretches of coastline from government forces, and the United Nations says more than 200,000 people have been displaced. Saudi Arabia has promised to retaliate firmly. A wider regional escalation is now a real risk, and it is one reason oil traders are nervous.

In Ukraine, President Trump announced on Sunday that Russia and Ukraine had agreed to an immediate energy ceasefire, halting attacks on each other's energy facilities. President Volodymyr Zelensky said it was news to him, but that Ukraine would agree if Russia does, and that Kyiv would stop hitting Russian refineries if Moscow stopped striking Ukrainian energy sites. On a US television program, Zelensky also offered to hold elections if Russia accepts a total ceasefire of 60 days. There has been no confirmation from the Kremlin. Trump made a similar announcement a month ago, and the strikes continued. The announcement followed a row over a United States deal to ease sanctions so Russian diesel can reach world markets. Trump blamed Ukrainian drone strikes on Russian refineries for high diesel prices and said on Saturday that Ukraine should get a new president. Zelensky called the diesel deal a weak decision by strong partners, and said Russia answered with strikes on homes in Zaporizhzhia that killed twelve people, including three children. Analysts doubt the diesel deal will lower prices much.

Now economy and society. Oil is back at the center of the story. Iran's Revolutionary Guards say a supertanker hit a naval mine in the Strait of Hormuz, the third tanker since Saturday that they say used an unauthorized route and caught fire. That claim comes from the Guards alone. CNN counts more than a dozen strikes on shipping since late September, keeping crude above one hundred dollars a barrel, and Brent jumped nearly five percent to over 105 dollars last week. The ten-year Treasury yield touched about 5.3 percent. This week, US September inflation data lands on Wednesday, with forecasters expecting headline inflation near 3.7 percent. Gulf exports have recovered to more than 80 percent of pre-war levels, helped by pipelines that now carry about forty percent of Gulf crude around the strait. Yet insurers and shipowners remain wary, and markets now expect the Federal Reserve to hold rates at its late October meeting. Higher fuel costs reach everyone, from airlines to your local gas station.

On society, the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize has gone to Navi Pillay, the South African jurist and former United Nations human rights chief. The Norwegian Nobel Committee, announcing the prize on Friday, cited her work to promote peace and international law. Pillay was a judge at the International Criminal Tribunal for Rwanda, was active in the anti-apartheid movement, and recently led the UN commission that concluded Israel committed genocide in Gaza. She becomes the fifth South African laureate. The prize will be presented in Oslo on December 10. Committee chair Jorgen Watne Frydnes called her one of the great defenders of international legal principles of our time, and said the system of international law is under tremendous pressure. UN human rights chief Volker Turk welcomed the award, while Israel's ambassador in Geneva called the decision disgraceful. CNN notes that President Trump has repeatedly said he deserved the prize himself.

Now sport. In Formula One, Max Verstappen won the Singapore Grand Prix on Sunday, his first victory at Marina Bay and the 73rd of his career, in his 250th start. Charles Leclerc finished second, 7.3 seconds behind, and Mercedes driver Kimi Antonelli took third. Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri were fourth and fifth. The bigger story is the championship. Antonelli leads his teammate George Russell by 102 points with six rounds left, and could clinch the title at the United States Grand Prix in two weeks, which would make him the youngest champion in the sport's history, at just over twenty years old. Russell's challenge has been hit by a late engine failure in Malaysia and a crash in the Singapore sprint. Lewis Hamilton was sixth in Singapore after a strategy error cost him a podium, and Verstappen has climbed to fourth in the standings.

In the NFL, the Green Bay Packers crushed the Chicago Bears thirty-one to seven at Lambeau Field on Sunday. Quarterback Jordan Love completed 25 of 31 passes for 367 yards and two touchdowns, and Christian Watson caught two touchdowns for 143 yards. Chicago lost the turnover battle five to zero and managed just 81 rushing yards. Both teams are now three and two, while Minnesota leads the division at five and zero. In women's football, world champions Spain beat the United States two to nil in Washington on Saturday, with both goals inside 14 minutes. The teams meet again on Tuesday.

Now health. Genmab and AbbVie announced on October 5 that adding epcoritamab to the standard R-CHOP chemotherapy regimen cut the risk of progression or death by 51 percent in newly diagnosed diffuse large B-cell lymphoma, the most common aggressive lymphoma. In the phase three EPCORE DLBCL-2 trial, patients were randomized two to one, and the hazard ratio was 0.49, with a very strong statistical result. The companies have not yet shown survival or safety data, which will matter, and the combination is not yet approved. If confirmed, it could change first-line treatment for a cancer that is curable in many, but not all, patients.

Finally, science. A British-led team reports in Nature Astronomy a possible second-generation planet around a white dwarf about 270 light-years away in the constellation Cetus. The star's atmosphere carries the rare element niobium at more than a thousand times the level in our Sun. Archival satellite data show a repeating dip in brightness every 4.4 days, suggesting a Jupiter-sized gas giant that formed from the debris of the star's death and is rapidly losing its atmosphere. The lead author stresses it is only a candidate for now. Separately, researchers describe a star system about 300 light-years away where a small star is slowly consuming a nearby brown dwarf. And a team using artificial intelligence on a large sky survey has found 70 new gravitational lenses, cosmic magnifying glasses that help map dark matter. These are early reports, but together they show how fast astronomers are expanding the catalog of strange systems.

## [fr] L'actualité de la France

Passons à l'actualité de la France.

En politique, la gauche a désigné son champion pour 2027. Raphaël Glucksmann a remporté dès le premier tour la primaire de la gauche sociale-démocrate, dimanche, avec 66,13 pour cent des suffrages exprimés. Il devance largement Olivier Faure, qui obtient 14,48 pour cent, et aucun second tour n'aura lieu. Un peu plus de 120 000 électeurs ont participé, malgré une cyberattaque qui a perturbé le vote. Raphaël Glucksmann avait annoncé sa candidature à la présidentielle le 23 août. Cette victoire nette lui donne une légitimité forte, mais la participation reste modeste, et il lui faudra maintenant convaincre bien au-delà de son camp. Reste à savoir comment les autres formations de gauche, et notamment les écologistes et La France insoumise, se positionneront face à lui dans les mois qui viennent.

Côté économie, le prix du carburant reste un sujet brûlant. Le gazole se vendait en moyenne autour de 2,35 euros le litre en fin de semaine, et le gouvernement a annoncé, mercredi, la libération de dix millions de barils de gazole issus des stocks stratégiques. Il promet une baisse de 12 à 18 centimes par litre, de novembre à janvier. Cette mesure ne concerne pas l'essence, et l'État annonce des contrôles renforcés pour éviter que les distributeurs n'en profitent pour gonfler leurs marges. Autre inquiétude, chez Gifi, enseigne de bazar d'environ 600 magasins selon franceinfo, où les représentants du personnel ont lancé un droit d'alerte économique face à des tensions de trésorerie. Le comité social et économique a voté ce droit d'alerte le 6 octobre, après la fermeture de onze magasins. La procédure oblige la direction à expliquer sa situation financière et à présenter un projet d'avenir. Les élus s'inquiètent aussi du paiement des fournisseurs et de départs en cascade. Pour les milliers de salariés, l'incertitude est grande.

Dans la société, la campagne annuelle de vaccination contre la grippe et le Covid-19 démarre mardi, 13 octobre. Environ 24,5 millions de doses sont disponibles : 15 millions contre la grippe, contre 11,4 millions l'an dernier, et 9,5 millions contre le Covid-19. La vaccination contre la grippe est prise en charge à 100 pour cent pour les personnes de 65 ans et plus, les malades chroniques et les femmes enceintes. Le vaccin contre le Covid reste gratuit pour tous. Environ 21 millions de personnes ont reçu un courrier d'invitation, et l'Assurance maladie va aussi appeler 250 000 assurés fragiles. La campagne durera jusqu'à fin janvier.

Côté culture, le cinéma est à la fête à Lyon. La dix-huitième édition du Festival Lumière a été lancée samedi soir à la Halle Tony Garnier, avec la projection d'Une journée particulière, d'Ettore Scola, avec Sophia Loren et Marcello Mastroianni. Le festival se tient jusqu'au 18 octobre. Le Prix Lumière sera remis vendredi aux frères Joel et Ethan Coen, qui succèdent à Michael Mann. Ils donneront une master class au théâtre des Célestins, avant la cérémonie au Centre de Congrès et la projection d'Inside Llewyn Davis. Une rétrospective de leur œuvre est au programme.

En sport, les Bleues ont fait carton plein samedi à Laval. Pour leur match amical, elles ont écrasé la Lettonie, 9 buts à 0, avec huit buteuses différentes. Kadidiatou Diani a ouvert le score dès la septième minute, et Anaële Le Moguédec a marqué deux fois. Grace Geyoro, Melvine Malard, Océane Hurtré, Kelly Gago, Clara Mateo et Marie-Antoinette Katoto ont aussi trouvé le chemin des filets. C'est la plus large victoire de l'équipe de France depuis septembre 2022. Le sélectionneur Laurent Bonadei a profité de la rencontre pour faire tourner son effectif. Les Bleues se retrouveront fin novembre, avec la Coupe du monde 2027 au Brésil en ligne de mire.

Toujours en sport, la sixième journée du Top 14 a offert de beaux scénarios. Samedi, Toulon a battu le Racing 92, 29 à 15, et le Stade Français a dominé Montpellier, 30 à 22. À Clermont, Bordeaux-Bègles s'est imposé d'un petit point, 23 à 22. Dimanche soir, le Stade Toulousain a pris le meilleur sur Bayonne, 45 à 34, avec le point de bonus offensif, et retrouve la tête du classement. Le championnat est serré, et chaque point compte déjà.

Voilà pour l'actualité française : Glucksmann s'impose à gauche, le gazole devrait baisser en novembre et la vaccination commence mardi. C'est la fin de cette édition. Merci de votre écoute, Elep, et excellente journée. À demain.
