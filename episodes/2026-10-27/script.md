## [vi] Tin tức trong nước

Chào Elep, hôm nay là thứ Ba, ngày hai mươi bảy tháng Mười năm hai nghìn không trăm hai mươi sáu. Chúc anh một chuyến đi làm thật êm. Mình bắt đầu với các con số của thị trường.

Khối ngoại bán ròng khoảng tám trăm chín mươi tỷ đồng trên cả ba sàn, riêng cổ phiếu FPT bị bán mạnh nhất.

Đây là con số suy ra từ mức giảm phần trăm, chưa phải giá niêm yết chính thức. Điểm sáng là dòng tiền: khối ngoại mua ròng khoảng sáu mươi bốn tỷ đồng cổ phiếu TCB, còn tự doanh các công ty chứng khoán mua ròng khoảng năm mươi mốt tỷ đồng, cao nhất trong các mã được tự doanh mua. ## [en] World news

Good morning. Let me start with the story that dominated Monday's business news. Anthropic has publicly filed its registration statement with the US Securities and Exchange Commission for an initial public offering. The company plans to list its Class A shares on the Nasdaq under the ticker A N T. The filing shows about sixteen point three billion dollars in revenue for the first half of the year. It also shows a net loss of roughly eighty point seven billion dollars over the same period, which reports say comes mostly from accounting charges rather than cash spending. The prospectus points to commitments of around four hundred seventeen billion dollars in future payments for computing power. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are named as underwriters. The share count and price range are not set yet, and a report from Fox Business says pricing could come as soon as November nineteenth or twentieth. Chip stocks rose on the news. Until the registration becomes effective, no shares can be sold.

Now to telecom. Comcast reported that it lost about one hundred forty-four thousand broadband customers in the third quarter. The company says it will restructure, with a charge of about one billion dollars expected in the fourth quarter and layoffs to follow. Fiber and fixed wireless rivals keep taking share from the cable giant, and that is the real story here.

Also in telecom, Nokia and the Finnish satellite company ICEYE signed an agreement to explore a sovereign low earth orbit satellite communications network for Ukraine. Separately, the operator group VEON says about eleven million customers in Ukraine, Kazakhstan and Bangladesh now use Starlink direct to device service on their phones. That is a useful number, because it shows satellite to phone is already a mass market product outside the United States.

Turning to politics. In Brazil, Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva has won a fourth term as president. With more than ninety-six percent of votes counted in Sunday's runoff, he leads Flávio Bolsonaro by roughly fifty-one and a half percent to forty-eight and a half. It is a narrow win in a bitterly divided country.

On Ukraine, the local ceasefire around the occupied town of Oleshky is under strain before it has even started. Russia's defence ministry accused Ukraine on Monday of repeated artillery and drone strikes on the town. Reuters could not verify the claim, and Ukraine has not responded. Reports differ on the start date, either October twenty-seventh or twenty-ninth. Technical talks between Ukraine, Russia and the United States are planned for the first half of November in Abu Dhabi.

In energy markets, Brent crude slipped about one point three percent on Monday to around one hundred fifteen dollars a barrel. West Texas Intermediate fell to about one hundred two dollars. Reports say crude shipments through the Strait of Hormuz have recovered to close to pre-war levels, and Saudi Aramco says its East-West pipeline, which bypasses the strait, now carries up to seven million barrels a day. President Trump accused Iran of exaggerating attacks on ships to push prices up before the midterm elections. Meanwhile, spot gold rose to around four thousand and twenty dollars an ounce.

In sport, Paris Saint-Germain beat Lyon two nil at the Parc des Princes on Monday night. Both goals came from substitutes, Senny Mayulu in the seventy-second minute and Mika Godts in the eighty-fourth. It was PSG's fifth straight league win, and it leaves them alone at the top with seventeen points. In baseball, the Milwaukee Brewers lead the World Series two games to none after a five to four comeback win over the Tampa Bay Rays in Game Two, having trailed by four runs. Game Three is in Tampa Bay.

That is the world for today. Now, to France.

## [fr] L'actualité de la France

Bonjour. Premier sujet, la crise lycéenne. Lundi, les ministres Édouard Geffray, pour l'Éducation nationale, et Philippe Baptiste, pour l'Enseignement supérieur, ont détaillé la réponse du gouvernement. Cinq cents postes seront créés dans les lycées, et mille deux cent soixante-quatre autres pour les BTS. Le nombre d'élèves par classe sera limité progressivement à vingt-huit en seconde d'ici la rentrée deux mille vingt-huit. Les huit cent trente-six suppressions de postes prévues dans le budget deux mille vingt-sept seraient annulées. Le plan prévoit aussi quinze mille places supplémentaires dans le supérieur et le remplacement de Parcoursup par un nouveau système. Reçues à Matignon par Sébastien Lecornu, les organisations de jeunesse jugent que le gouvernement a répondu à une partie seulement de leurs demandes. Elles appellent à poursuivre le mouvement, avec de nouvelles mobilisations les trente et un octobre et cinq novembre.

Côté économie, la Bourse de Paris a rebondi lundi. Le CAC 40 a gagné zéro virgule quatre-vingt-trois pour cent pour terminer à sept mille huit cent quarante-sept points. Les investisseurs ont profité d'une détente des taux d'emprunt à dix ans, après la décision de l'agence Moody's de ne pas dégrader la note de la France.

Au Parlement, le Sénat a commencé lundi à examiner le projet de loi constitutionnelle qui doit permettre une Corse autonome au sein de la République, un texte déjà adopté par l'Assemblée nationale. Il doit ensuite passer à la protection de l'enfance. À l'Assemblée, les débats sur la partie État du budget reprennent aujourd'hui.

Enfin, le sport. Au Parc des Princes, le Paris Saint-Germain a battu Lyon deux à zéro lundi soir, grâce à deux joueurs entrés en cours de jeu, Senny Mayulu et Mika Godts. Paris compte dix-sept points et prend seul la tête de la Ligue 1, devant Rennes, à seize points. Lyon, qui partageait la tête avant cette journée, tombe à la cinquième place avec quatorze points.

Voilà pour ce matin. Merci de votre écoute, Elep, et excellente journée. À demain.
