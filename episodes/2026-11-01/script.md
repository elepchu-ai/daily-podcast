## [en] World news

Good morning, Elep. Here is the world briefing for Sunday, the first of November.

Let me start with telecom. Ericsson and the Australian operator Telstra have expanded their partnership on autonomous networks. The announcement came at the start of Ericsson's customer event on its operations and business support software. The two companies will co-lead a joint working group built around what they call a knowledge plane. That is a formal description of what a network is, what it should be doing, and what it is expected to do, so that artificial intelligence agents can run networks with more trust. The work focuses on three areas: a common taxonomy for network knowledge, ways to exchange and combine that knowledge, and agents that act on it. It builds on the first program the two firms launched in October last year.

Also in telecom, a market report this year says that the five largest radio network suppliers, Huawei, Ericsson, Nokia, ZTE and Samsung, together took about ninety-six percent of global radio network revenue in the first half of the year. That shows how concentrated the business remains, even as operators talk about open and virtualized networks. On the Samsung side, its phones-and-networks unit lost money last quarter, as we reported yesterday, so the pressure on the smaller players is real.

Now to the Middle East. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said on Saturday that President Trump has not yet decided whether to resume strikes on Iran, and that he still prefers a diplomatic outcome. Mister Trump has said he is weighing a deal against action, and he spoke by phone with Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu as Washington considers its options. Iran's envoy to Pakistan said Tehran would reopen the Strait of Hormuz if the United States lifted its naval blockade of Iranian ports. Washington says the blockade stays for now. Iran's supreme leader, Mojtaba Khamenei, warned the United States and Israel against fresh attacks. The American military says the shipping lanes are open, while Iran says a tanker hit a mine east of the strait and that two other tankers on an unauthorized route were stopped.

In sport, the Premier League played on Saturday. Chelsea beat Manchester United two goals to nil. Tottenham beat Crystal Palace two to nil against a Palace side reduced to ten men. It was Tottenham's third straight win, the first such run since February last year.

Finally, a reminder on health: the Ebola outbreak caused by the Bundibugyo virus remains the main outbreak under watch by the World Health Organization.

## [fr] L'actualité de la France

Bonjour Elep, et bienvenue dans le point sur la France de ce dimanche premier novembre.

La grande nouvelle d'hier, c'est la troisième journée de mobilisation contre la vie chère. Selon le gouvernement, trois cent vingt mille personnes ont défilé samedi dans toute la France, dont soixante-dix mille à Paris. Les syndicats, eux, avancent des chiffres bien plus élevés : cinq cent mille manifestants, dont deux cent mille à Paris, selon l'intersyndicale de la jeunesse. Des heurts ont opposé policiers et manifestants à Paris après le cortège. Le ministère de l'Intérieur recense quatre cent vingt-deux interpellations dans le pays, contre près de mille lors de la précédente journée, le vingt-quatre octobre. À Paris, la préfecture de police compte cent quarante-deux interpellations, dont cent vingt-six gardes à vue. Avant la journée, les autorités avaient évoqué un risque de situation insurrectionnelle dans la capitale.

Du côté du football, Lyon recevait Angers samedi soir en Ligue un. Dimanche soir, Marseille affronte Toulouse pour un match à vingt heures quarante-cinq.

Côté budget, le calendrier reste serré. Le Parlement dispose de soixante-dix jours pour examiner le projet de loi de finances, soit jusqu'au vingt-six novembre. Le Premier ministre Sébastien Lecornu a promis de ne pas recourir au quarante-neuf trois s'il n'y a pas d'obstruction. Le gouvernement n'a pas de majorité, et La France insoumise a déjà annoncé qu'elle voterait la censure.

C'est tout pour ce matin. Merci de votre écoute, Elep, et excellente journée. À demain.
