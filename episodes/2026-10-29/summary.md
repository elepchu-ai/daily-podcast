## World
- Telecom: FCC votes today on a 25 MHz mid-band auction for satellite-to-phone service and on seeking comment on 482 MHz more; AT&T, Verizon and T-Mobile oppose. Qualcomm reports November 4 after weak July guidance.
- Fed holds at 3.75-4 percent in a 10-2 vote, both dissenters wanting a hike; markets price about 85 percent odds of a December 9 hike.
- Alphabet Q3: revenue up 25 percent to 127.6 billion dollars, Google Cloud roughly doubles to 30.7 billion, backlog 561 billion.
- Sport: Rays win Game 4 of the World Series 4-3 in 12 innings; Brewers lead 3-1.
- Sport: Liverpool beat Chelsea 6-5 on penalties after a 1-1 draw in the Carabao Cup.
- Ukraine: Kremlin says Zelensky can come to Moscow for talks; Putin floats a Xi-Trump-Putin meeting at APEC in November.
- Health: Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak passes 10,000 cases and 4,887 deaths, with cases in Kenya, Nigeria and Uganda.
